Mission & Programs

Why we exist, what we do, and what our community has accomplished together.

Our mission

The mission of the Somerset Academy Oaks PTO is to provide the resources to organize meaningful activities and events that strengthen our entire school community: students, teachers and staff, and families.

Our vision

Our vision is to support the Somerset Academy Oaks community through fundraising activities that are fun and engaging and further develop the physical, mental, emotional, and social well-being of all children and youth.

Our goal

Our primary goal is to promote the collaboration and engagement of families and educators in the education of children and youth.

Programs and projects

  • Big Give Fundraiser

    Our fall fundraiser. Families, friends, and supporters donate and share the campaign. Classes compete for rewards such as class parties, extra recess, and funds for classroom supplies.

  • Fun Day

    A schoolwide celebration held during recess, with activities on the turf field, popsicles, and music.

  • Fall Treat Day and Trunk or Treat

    Fall events for students and families. In 2026, Fall Treat Day was October 23 and Trunk or Treat was October 30.

  • Holiday Treasures Holiday Market

    An in-school holiday shop where students can buy gifts for family and friends during the school day. The 2026 market runs November 10-13 in the campus library and is also open to families before and after school and during the Fall Festival.

  • Lunch Assistant Program

    Parent volunteers help during the school lunch period. Volunteers complete a school background check before signing up.

Our impact

$14,945

raised in our fall 2026 Big Give Fundraiser, surpassing our stretch goal.

  • Surpassing the stretch goal allowed the PTO to open Fun Day to every student at Somerset Academy Oaks.
  • Top fundraising and participation classes earned class parties, extra recess, and money for classroom supplies.
  • Funds raised this year support activities, events, and resources for students, teachers and staff, and families throughout the 2026-2027 school year.
