Mission & Programs

What we do and where your support goes.

Our mission

The mission of the Somerset Academy Oaks PTO is to provide the resources to organize meaningful activities and events that strengthen our entire school community: students, teachers and staff, and families.

Put simply, we want Somerset Academy Oaks to be a place where families and teachers know each other and students have something to look forward to.

Our vision

Our vision is to support the Somerset Academy Oaks community through fundraising activities that are fun and engaging and further develop the physical, mental, emotional, and social well-being of all children and youth.

Our goal

Our primary goal is to promote the collaboration and engagement of families and educators in the education of children and youth.

What we do

  • Raise money for our school

    Our Big Give campaign brings families, friends, and neighbors together to fund the school year. Classes cheer each other on, and the students and classes earn prizes.

  • Bring everyone together

    We plan school-wide celebrations, seasonal events, and a holiday shop just for students, so students and families have reasons to be on campus together.

  • Back our teachers

    Teachers send us requests for what their classrooms and programs need, and the board votes on them. Every month, we also draw one teacher's name for a classroom supplies basket.

  • Volunteer opportunities

    From lunch duty to school events, we give families simple ways to volunteer, whether that's once a year or every week.
