Mission & Programs

Why we do this, and what it looks like on campus.

Our mission

The mission of the Somerset Academy Oaks PTO is to provide the resources to organize meaningful activities and events that strengthen our entire school community: students, teachers and staff, and families.

Put simply, we want Somerset Oaks to be a place where families and teachers know each other and kids have something to look forward to.

Our vision

Our vision is to support the Somerset Academy Oaks community through fundraising activities that are fun and engaging and further develop the physical, mental, emotional, and social well-being of all children and youth.

Our goal

Our primary goal is to promote the collaboration and engagement of families and educators in the education of children and youth.

What we do

  • Big Give Fundraiser

    Our fall fundraiser. Families, friends, and neighbors donate and share it around, and classes compete for prizes like an ice cream party, extra recess, and money for classroom supplies.

  • Fun Day

    Our thank-you to the kids: activities on the turf field, popsicles, and music during recess. This year every student got to go because the Big Give beat its stretch goal.

  • Fall Treat Day and Trunk or Treat

    Costumes, candy, and a campus full of excited kids. In 2026 we held Fall Treat Day on October 23 and Trunk or Treat on October 30.

  • Holiday Treasures

    An in-school holiday shop where Little Owls pick out gifts for their families on their own. This year it runs November 10-13 in the campus library. Families can shop before and after school, and in the cafeteria during the Fall Festival.

  • Monthly Teacher Basket Drawing

    Every month at our Executive Board meeting, we draw a teacher's name for a gift basket paid for with PTO funds. It's our small way of saying thanks.

  • Teacher Requests

    Teachers and staff send us requests for things their classrooms and programs need, and the Executive Board votes on them. In October every request was approved unanimously, so soccer goals, a lab microwave, and new sports equipment are on their way.

  • Lunch Assistant Program

    Parent volunteers who help out during the lunch rush. You'll need a cleared school background check before signing up.

Our impact

$14,945

That number came from families, friends, and supporters who donated, shared, and showed up for this fall's Big Give. Here's what it did right away:

  • Fun Day opened up to every student at Somerset Oaks.
  • Top classes earned an ice cream party, extra recess, and money for classroom supplies.
  • Every teacher request we received in October was approved unanimously, covering soccer goals, a lab microwave, and new sports equipment.
  • A teacher takes home a gift basket every month through our drawing.
  • The funds support events, activities, and resources for students, teachers and staff, and families through the 2026-2027 school year.
