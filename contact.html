Volunteer with us

You can help once or become a regular, and every bit of time counts. Lunch assistance, school events, and the Holiday Market all need people. Please make sure your school background check has cleared before you sign up.

We also have an open seat on the board: Membership & Volunteer Coordinator. If that sounds like you, email us.
