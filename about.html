About Us

Parents and staff who show up for Somerset Oaks.

Our story

We started as an informal group of Somerset Oaks parents and staff who wanted to give back to our school. For a couple of years we pitched in where we could, planning events for students and finding ways to support teachers.

In 2026 we became a registered nonprofit. That gave us bylaws, an Executive Board, and a more organized way to raise money and decide where it goes.

The reason we do it is the same as day one. We want our kids to love coming to school, and we want our teachers to know families have their backs.

How we're run

Our Executive Board meets every month to plan events, review teacher requests, and decide how PTO funds are spent. During the school year we also hold General Membership Meetings, open to every Somerset Oaks family and staff member.
