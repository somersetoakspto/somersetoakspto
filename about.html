About Us

A couple of years ago we were an informal group of parents and staff trying to make good things happen at our school. We still are. Now we're a registered nonprofit too.

Our story

The PTO started as an informal group at Somerset Academy Oaks. For a couple of years, parents and staff pitched in on events and school needs without a formal organization behind them.

In 2026 we made it official. We adopted bylaws, set up an Executive Board, and became a registered nonprofit. The IRS assigned us EIN 33-4284291, and 2026-2027 is our first school year as a nonprofit.

This fall was our first big test. Our Big Give Fundraiser brought in $14,945 and cleared our stretch goal, so we opened Fun Day to the whole school. Lunch Assistant volunteers are back in the cafeteria, and we're getting the Holiday Treasures shop ready for our Little Owls.

Our nonprofit status

Under our bylaws, the Somerset Academy Oaks Parent Teacher Organization is organized exclusively for charitable, scientific, literary, or educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

How we're run

Our Executive Board meets every month and manages the PTO's business under our bylaws. That's where we vote on teacher requests and draw the monthly teacher basket winner. We share updates with families at General Membership Meetings, and the next one is November 17, 2026. Come say hi.
