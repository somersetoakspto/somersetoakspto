Welcome, Owl families

We're the parents and staff behind the Somerset Academy Oaks PTO in San Antonio. We plan the fun stuff, raise money for our school, and look for ways to get families and teachers working together.

Who we are

We're a group of Somerset Academy Oaks parents and staff. For a couple of years we ran things informally, and in 2026 we became a registered nonprofit. The money we raise goes to activities, events, and resources for our students, teachers and staff, and families.

This fall

$14,945

That's what our Owl community raised in this year's Big Give Fundraiser. We didn't expect a response like that, and it carried us past our stretch goal. In October, the board also approved every teacher request we received, including soccer goals, a lab microwave, and new sports equipment. See more of what PTO funds are doing.

Want to help?

Help once, or sign up as a regular. Lunch assistance, school events, and the Holiday Market all need volunteers. Drop us a line at info@somersetoakspto.org or see the Contact page.
