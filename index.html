Welcome to the Somerset Academy Oaks PTO

We organize activities, events, and fundraising that strengthen our school community: students, teachers and staff, and families.

Who we are

The Somerset Academy Oaks Parent Teacher Organization (PTO) is a nonprofit organization run by parent and staff volunteers. We raise funds and organize activities and events for the students, teachers and staff, and families of Somerset Academy Oaks in San Antonio, Texas.
